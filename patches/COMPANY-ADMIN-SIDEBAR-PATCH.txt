Add this standalone item to the existing Company Admin sidebar:

<button
  type="button"
  onClick={() => router.push("/company-admin/accountant-bridge")}
  title="Accountant Verification Bridge"
>
  <span className="material-symbols-rounded">difference</span>
  <span>Accountant Verification Bridge</span>
</button>

This page lets Company Admin:
- record the Company Admin half of expense approval;
- attach a reference message/file for Staff proof and SMS verification;
- attach the official bank statement values and file;
- register or enrol approved fingerprint devices.

Compatibility alias also included:
/admin/accountant-bridge
