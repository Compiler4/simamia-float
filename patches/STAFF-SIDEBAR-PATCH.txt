Add this standalone item to the existing Staff sidebar:

<button
  type="button"
  onClick={() => router.push("/staff/verification-center")}
  title="Proof and Verification Center"
>
  <span className="material-symbols-rounded">fact_check</span>
  <span>Proof & Verification Center</span>
</button>

This page lets Staff:
- upload proof, documents or SMS/message evidence;
- upload bank deposit proof;
- see whether Company Admin has supplied a reference;
- see the final Accountant verification or rejection and reason.
